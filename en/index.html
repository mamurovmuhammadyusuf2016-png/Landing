Beginner Arabic (phonetics)

The starting stage for complete beginners. You learn to read Arabic script and to pronounce the sounds that do not exist in your own language â without that there is neither confident reading nor clear speech.

  • The Arabic alphabet and every letter form
  • Getting the difficult sounds right and working on articulation
  • Vowel marks and reading aloud without transcription
  • First phrases and the vocabulary for simple conversation

For adults

Grammar

The stage after phonetics. We take the language apart and apply the rules in speech straight away, so that you build sentences yourself instead of memorising ready-made phrases.

  • Parts of speech, gender, number and case
  • The verb: tenses, forms and conjugation
  • Building sentences, from simple to complex
  • Speaking practice and error analysis in every lesson
